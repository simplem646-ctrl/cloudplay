## Known issues (honest list, final)
- **Follow-camera edges (not fixed).** `src/world/camera.js clampTarget` keeps the whole view footprint inside the area (+1 tile), so near the north/south edges the camera stops while the player keeps walking: measured in the browser, **13-17% of the walkable cells of every exterior area are outside the frame** (north and south strips up to ~6-7 tiles deep, 2-3 tile strips at east/west); and the player can stand hidden behind tall buildings (e.g. the north strip of `town_square` behind the town hall). Area exits at the edges (`to_whispering_woods@15,0`, `to_starview_hill@23,0`, `to_riverside_park@20,29`, ...) are reached by walking out of frame for the last steps. Relaxing the clamp by 5 tiles showed only a thin sky band at the top of `brook_lane`, so overscan + a scenery skirt or an inset walk boundary (keeping exit corridors) are the options; dithered see-through for occluding buildings would address the second problem. The clamp-invariant tests in `tests/unit/world-*.test.mjs` would need updating.
- Not verified: audio never heard; real touch device / gamepad; real safe-area insets; real-hardware frame rate (sandbox software GL ≈ 4 fps); narration cancel keys in a browser; the new talk-pool conditions `{night}`, `{lanterns}`, `{all}` live; staging of festival scenes and of interior events (only endings, Juno 8, Mira 6, Gus friend 1 were watched; all other exterior heart/friend events were checked numerically, not by eye).
- Content is modest for a "story-driven" game (endings ~28 steps, heart events ~10-20 steps, ~15-19 talk entries per romance character, 4-9 for ordinary villagers; ordinary-villager pools were not extended). The simulation teleports instead of walking and uses fake minigames. An NPC in front of a noticeboard/counter wins the prompt (use the NPC menu entry). `src/data/tutorial.js` still says "Give one gift a day to a friend".
