# Lanternbrook Days — How to Play

A cosy little town story. You have just moved into the Old Lamplighter's Cottage on Brook Lane in the seaside town of
Lanternbrook. The street lanterns have been dark since the old lamplighter passed away, and the Winter Lantern Festival has not
been held since. There is no farming here, no timer to beat and no way to lose. Make friends, take a job, go fishing, and help
the town light its lanterns again.

## Starting out

1. Click the game once (so the browser sends it your keys and lets it play sound), then press any key.
2. Choose **New Game**. Type a name (up to 10 letters, "Kai" by default) and pick a hair, jacket and scarf colour.
3. Choose **Begin your story**. The mayor meets you outside the cottage; press **Space** or **Enter** to read on.

You can do all of this with the keyboard alone: Up/Down move between rows, Left/Right change a colour, Space/Enter confirm,
X or Esc go back.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| Move | Arrow keys or **W A S D** (8 directions) |
| Run | Hold **Shift** |
| Talk / use / confirm | **Space**, **Enter**, **Z** or **F** |
| Back / cancel | **X** or **Backspace** |
| Open the menu | **Esc** or **Tab** |
| Items / Journal / Map | **I** / **J** / **M** |
| Switch menu tabs | **Q** and **E** (or **[** and **]**) |
| Hold confirm | Speeds up dialogue text |

- **Mouse or touch:** click or tap anything on screen. On touch devices a thumb stick appears on the left, with **A**, **B** and
  **Run** buttons on the right and a menu button at the top.
- **Gamepad:** left stick or d-pad to move, **A** confirm, **B** cancel, **Start** menu, **LB/RB** tabs, **X** run.

Whenever something can be used, a small prompt appears at the bottom of the screen (for example **Space: Talk**). Walk up to it
and press the key. The clock pauses while you are in a conversation, a menu, a shop or a mini-game.

## The goal: rekindle the lanterns

Tobias Wren, the lamplighter who lived in your cottage, left a journal. It tells the stories of six townspeople who loved him.
Help each of them with a small favour and you will be given a **Lantern Piece**; each one lets the street lamps of a district be
repaired and lit again. Gather them all and you can build the **Great Lantern**.

Everything builds to the **Lantern Festival on Winter 24**, when the whole town gathers in the square. The more lanterns you
have restored by then, the more magical the evening is. You never miss out for being slow: unfinished stories just make the
festival a little simpler. The game keeps going afterwards.

Your journal (**J**) always shows what to do next. The **Map** (**M**) shows where everyone and everything is.

## A day in Lanternbrook

- You wake at **6:00 AM** in the cottage. Check the mailbox out front; letters arrive most days.
- **Time passes while you walk around:** about 2 game minutes per real second (change this in Settings).
- Try to be in bed by **midnight**. You sleep fully rested if you do. If you stay up later you wake a bit tired, and at **2:00 AM**
  you collapse where you are and wake up at home with less energy (and a few coins lighter). It is only a small setback.
- **Energy** (the flame in the corner) is spent by jobs, fishing and foraging. Eating food, sitting on a bench and sleeping
  all restore it. Running costs nothing.
- At the bed, choose **Sleep**. A summary of the day appears, the game autosaves and a new day begins.
- The week has a rhythm: shops have opening hours and closed days, villagers follow their own timetables, and a **travelling
  peddler** visits on weekends. The weather changes (tomorrow's forecast is in the day summary).

## Ways to spend your time

| What | Where / when | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Bakery helper** | The kneading table at the bakery, Mon to Sat mornings (7-11) | A rhythm mini-game: knead the dough on the beat. Pay depends on your score. |
| **Parcel runner** | The post counter in the general store, Mon to Sat afternoons (1-5) | Deliver parcels to the right villagers before the deadline. Learn who is where, and when. |
| **Lamplighter** | The lamp ledger in the town hall, weekday evenings from 5:30 PM | Walk the lamps and light them. Only lamps that have been repaired can be lit. |
| **Fishing** | Any fishing spot, any time | You need a rod (you get a starter rod early on). Cast, wait for a bite, then keep the marker in the bar. Fish depend on the water, season, weather and time of day. |
| **Foraging** | Foraging spots in the woods, fields and beaches | They refill every day. Seasons change what you find. |
| **Stargazing and reading** | The telescope at night, the library shelves | Calm little scenes; some are linked to people's stories. |
| **Errands** | The notice board and villagers | Small favours for coins and friendship. |

## Friends, hearts and gifts

- Talk to people: the first conversation of each day with someone makes you closer. Later chats that day are just friendly.
- Closeness is shown as **hearts** (0 to 10) in the **People** tab.
- **Gifts:** choose "Give a gift" when you talk to someone and pick an item from your pack. You can give one gift per person per
  day, and a lot of gifts in one week will have less effect. Everybody has favourites, things they like, and things they would
  rather not receive. When someone asks for several of an item for a quest, you can hand over everything they need at once (it counts as that day's gift). Their birthdays (see the **Calendar** tab) make a good gift count for much more.
- At certain heart levels, special scenes with a villager may take place. Five villagers can become more than friends.
  Only one partner at a time; the choice is entirely yours.

## Shops and hours

Shops and buildings keep opening hours and are closed one day a week. The sign on a closed door tells you when it opens, and the
**Calendar** tab lists what is on. Highlights: the general store (tools, bait, basics), bakery, café, clinic, inn, fishing hut,
and the flower stall in the town square. Choose **Buy** or **Sell** at the top of a shop, pick an item, use Left/Right for the
amount and press Space or Enter. The workshop stays locked until the story opens it.

## Saving

- **The diary** in the cottage (use it like any other object) saves your game.
- The **pause menu** (**Esc**, then the **Settings** tab) has three manual slots, plus **Export save** and **Import save** to back
  up or move a save as text.
- The game **autosaves every time you sleep**. The title screen's **Continue** lists every slot.
- Saves live in **this browser's local storage** for this address. They stay when you close the tab, but not if you clear site
  data, use a private window, or open the game somewhere else. Use **Export save** to keep a copy. If your browser refuses
  to store anything, the game still runs but your progress only lasts until you close the page.

## Settings

Open the menu and choose **Settings**: master volume, music, sound effects and ambience; text speed; time speed (slow,
normal or fast); graphics quality (low, medium or high); and touch controls (auto, on or off). They are remembered for next time.
**Quit to title** is there too.

## Festivals and seasonal days

Lanternbrook keeps a calendar. Look for these and plan an evening around them:

- **Blossom Picnic**, Spring 14
- **Midsummer Lights**, Summer 21
- **Autumn Moon Fair**, Autumn 14
- **Hearthlight Evening**, Winter 10
- **The Lantern Festival**, Winter 24

Festival days are always fair weather, and a few buildings close so everybody can attend.

## Troubleshooting

- **The screen stays black or the game says it could not start:** the game needs **WebGL**. Use a current Chrome, Edge, Firefox or
  Safari, make sure hardware acceleration is on, and reload the page.
- **Keys do nothing or there is no sound:** click on the game once. Browsers only give a page the keyboard and allow sound
  after you interact with it.
- **It runs slowly or the fans spin up:** add **`?q=low`** to the end of the address (for example `…/index.html?q=low`) or set
  Quality to Low in Settings. A smaller browser window helps too, and so does closing other tabs.
- **The game seems to pause when you switch tabs:** that is on purpose. It stops while the tab is hidden and carries on when
  you come back.
- **Lost a save:** see *Saving* above; saves belong to one browser and address.
- **On a phone or tablet:** hold the device sideways. If the on-screen controls do not show up, set Touch controls to On in Settings.
